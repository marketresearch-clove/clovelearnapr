- Main cable runs: Kabelfuehrung am Mast series
- Cable trays with 500mm bracket spacing

- Grounding bonding straps @ 2m intervals

#### D. Electrical Distribution (Stromverteilung)

- RRU converter output: -14V DC
- Backup: Battery-backed UPS system
- Power capacity: ~2 kW peak system

#### E. Grounding & Lightning Protection (Blitzschutz)
